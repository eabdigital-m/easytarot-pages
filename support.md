# Easy Tarot: Support

Need help or have a suggestion? Email **support@eabdigital.com** and we'll reply as soon as we can.

## Frequently asked

**Where is my progress saved?** On your device only. It isn't synced between devices.

**How do I reset my progress?** Today → gear icon → Reset learning progress. Your journal is kept.

**Can I turn off reversed cards?** Yes: Today → gear icon → Include reversed cards.

**Does Easy Tarot predict the future?** No. Easy Tarot is for learning, reflection and entertainment.

Card artwork: the original 1909 deck illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith (public domain).
