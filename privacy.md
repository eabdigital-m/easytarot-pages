# Easy Tarot: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

Easy Tarot ("the app") is published by EAB Digital. We respect your privacy.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and requires no account.

## Data stored on your device

Your learning progress, settings and any readings you save to the journal are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
